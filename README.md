# A Little Love Letter

This little website began as a vibe-coding experiment: an opportunity to turn an
idea into something personal by building with AI and seeing where a bit of
creative curiosity could lead.

But the real reason I made it is Amarrah.

I'm RJ, and I'm in the UAE while Amarrah is in Florida. Long distance can mean
many miles and time zones between two people, so I wanted to make her something
she could open from anywhere: a small reminder that she is loved, missed, and
always close to my heart.

It's a simple page, made with love - and a little blush pink.

## Open the page

Open `index.html` in a browser to view the page on your computer. To share it
with Amarrah as a link, publish the site with a static website host such as
GitHub Pages.
